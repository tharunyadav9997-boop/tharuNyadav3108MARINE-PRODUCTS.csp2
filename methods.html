Processing Methods

How are they processed?

Marine products are highly perishable. Proper processing is essential to increase their shelf life and maintain nutritional value.

  • 1. Sun Drying: The oldest method, removing moisture using sunlight.
  • 2. Salting: Adding salt to draw out water and stop bacterial growth.
  • 3. Freezing: Rapidly lowering temperature to keep products fresh.
  • 4. Canning: Sealing products in airtight containers and heating them.

"Proper processing prevents waste and ensures food security for millions worldwide."
